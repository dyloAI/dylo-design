The mono, wide-tracked section label with a leading oxide tick — place it directly above a headline to open a section; it's the most recognizable dylo device.

```jsx
<Eyebrow>The idea</Eyebrow>
<Eyebrow tone="light">Voice & tone</Eyebrow>   {/* on ink surfaces */}
<Eyebrow tick={false}>Appendix</Eyebrow>
```

Always uppercase content. Use `tone="light"` on dark/ink backgrounds. The oxide tick is the brand's one accent for that view — don't add another.
