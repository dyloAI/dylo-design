## The rhythm that carries it

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Daily** | Clear the dylo queue — nothing ends the day in `sin_triar`. 2 discovery calls. Send the queued demo drafts |
| **Mon** | Scorecard vs the plan's month row: calls, closes, builds, MRR, cash, hours per build, cost per held call by channel |
| **Wed** | Demo batch — personalize tier-2 demos for anyone who engaged last week. Protect this hour |
| **Fri** | Fleet sweep (Sentry, uptime, security deps). **Invoice everything delivered that week** — never batch to month end |
| **1st Mon** | Close the month: margin per SKU, collections over 30 days, client status notes |
| **Quarterly** | Review the plan against actuals and revise the assumptions |

## The three numbers to watch above all others

1. **Discovery calls per month.** At 20 instead of 40, the year lands ~45% lower.
2. **Human hours per build.** 9h supports ~110 builds/year; 16h supports 62 and
   misses by ~€80k. Currently unmeasured — which is why logging starts 08-28.
3. **Maintenance attach rate.** 55% gives €6,200/mo exit MRR; 25% opens a €40k hole.
